# Changelog

All notable changes to BD Marketplace are documented here.

## [2.0.0] — 2026-11-01

### Storefront redesign (Daraz-inspired)
- New orange marketplace header with utility strip, search category selector,
  wishlist/messages/cart shortcuts, and account menu.
- Category mega menu and horizontal department navigation.
- Auto-rotating hero carousel with side promo cards and dot/arrow controls.
- Circular category navigation with emoji icons and pastel tints.
- Flash-sale rail with live countdown, discount badges, and stock/sold progress bars.
- "Just For You" feed with Featured / New arrivals / Best sellers tabs and load-more.
- Redesigned product cards: discount corner badge, compact BDT price, compare price,
  ratings, sold count, and COD tag.
- Redesigned listing page: breadcrumb, result bar, sort dropdown, filter rail, active
  filter chips, and pagination.
- Restyled product detail price panel with payment/returns badges.
- Mobile-first header, two-column product grid, and slide-down category panel.
- New footer with payment badges (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Bangla QR, COD), support
  contacts, newsletter, and policy links.

### Platform
- Brand colour, typography, content width, card radius, and product-column settings
  applied at runtime across the storefront.
- Default product grid raised to five columns for wide screens.
- Updated storefront metadata and Open Graph-ready title.
- Comprehensive setup README, screenshots, MIT license, and changelog.
- CI continues to run 111 backend tests and the frontend type-check/build.

## [1.0.0]

- Initial multi-vendor marketplace: catalog, cart, checkout, COD and manual wallet
  payments, seller centre, admin console, receipts, email/SMS, plugins, and reports.
